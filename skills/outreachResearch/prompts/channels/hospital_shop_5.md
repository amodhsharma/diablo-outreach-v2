WHO COUNTS
Operators of shops and kiosks in hospitals, clinics and care homes that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks and sell them to patients, visitors and staff as they are. Many are run by retail or kiosk chains, hospital shop operators or charities that buy for several sites, so the buying company is the target. Sugar free and no added sugar sweets suit these customers well.

LEAVE OUT
Hospital and care home kitchens, catering companies and food service suppliers whose customers cook or serve meals (rule 7), medical and pharmacy suppliers, single hospitals that do not run their own shop, and anything the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
Hospital and clinic websites (their shop, kiosk or "shopping" pages name the operator), hospital shop and kiosk operator websites, care home group websites, healthcare retail trade press, and local business listings searched with words such as "Spitalkiosk", "kiosque hôpital" or "hospital shop".

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a company that runs shops or kiosks in several hospitals or care homes with a sweets, chocolate and snacks shelf of other brands. Prioritise: number of sites; central buying; already stocks sugar free or better-for-you sweets without a direct competitor.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rule 7: a shop or kiosk that sells packs to patients, visitors and staff as they are is not a supplier to kitchens; catering and meal services are still out. Rule 4: it must buy stock and sell it from its own shops; no storage line is needed. Rule 5: its shops or website must show sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks for sale. All the other rules apply in full.
