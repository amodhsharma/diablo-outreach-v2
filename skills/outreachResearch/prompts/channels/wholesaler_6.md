WHO COUNTS
Big wholesalers, cash and carry operators and wholesale buying groups that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks in bulk, hold them in their own warehouses, depots or cash and carry stores and sell them as they are to many retailers, kiosks, petrol station shops, convenience stores, vending operators or pharmacies across a region or the whole country. They may buy from importers rather than import directly. Wholesalers that run a symbol group, act as a purchasing centre for independent grocers or supply a national network of kiosks and convenience stores count fully, and so do confectionery and kiosk wholesalers of that size.

LEAVE OUT
Retailers that sell only to consumers, brand owners, exporters and logistics-only providers. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock. Foodservice wholesalers whose customers are kitchens. Small wholesalers that serve only a town or a handful of shops, and sweet shops that also sell a little wholesale (rule 12). Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
The national wholesalers' association (for example ABAD in Brazil, the Federation of Wholesale Distributors in the UK) and its rankings of the largest wholesalers, symbol group and buying group member lists, kiosk and convenience trade associations, cash and carry directories, B2B web shops for retailers, the trade press and trade show exhibitor lists. Also the brand owners' distributor pages (SEARCH THROUGH THE BRANDS FIRST). In a large country, search state by state. Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a wholesaler that lists many branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits and snacks from other companies, holds them in its own depots or cash and carry stores and supplies thousands of independent grocers, kiosks and convenience stores across its region or country (in Diablo's own list: Ch. Margot & Cie, Cadar, Spanish Market, Distribuciones Delfín, Sefalana Cash & Carry, Unimarka). Prioritise: national or regional reach; lists European or UK branded confectionery and snacks; supplies many independent convenience, kiosk, grocery or pharmacy stores, or runs a symbol group; already carries sugar-free, no-added-sugar or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
All twelve apply in full. Rule 4: write how the wholesaler holds stock after "Storage: " (warehouse, depot, cash and carry stores, own fleet or B2B web shop). Rule 7: cash and carry and foodservice wholesalers count only when they sell branded packaged confectionery or snacks for resale, not only catering packs. Rule 8: an ethnic wholesaler counts only with its own sweets or snacks section and shop customers. Rule 12: write what shows its size after "Scale: " (customers or outlets, cash and carry stores or depots, warehouse size, fleet, coverage).
