WHO COUNTS
Companies that match the channel "{{CATEGORY_AS_TYPED}}" as written, and that buy packaged food or confectionery and hold it in stock to resell in the market. Read the channel's words literally. When unsure whether a company fits, leave it out or mark it low confidence.

LEAVE OUT
Brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and competitors that make sugar-free products. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock. Any company whose food or confectionery range you could not confirm. Companies that sell nothing from Diablo's catalogue, that mainly sell their own products or own label, or that supply ingredients and semi-finished products to professionals.

WHERE TO LOOK
Trade association and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass), the country's grocery and convenience trade press, trade show exhibitor lists, company registries, retailer supplier pages and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). Use a directory only to find a company, then confirm it on the company's own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise, in this order: handles European or UK branded confectionery and snacks; a mid-size business where a decision maker can be reached; named access to retail, pharmacy, convenience or online channels in the market; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
All nine apply as far as they fit the channel as typed. Rule 4: a company that sells to the trade must have its own warehouse or depot (write it after "Storage: " in the fit rationale); a company that sells to shoppers must buy stock and sell it from its own shops or online shop. When unsure whether a rule is met, reject the company.
