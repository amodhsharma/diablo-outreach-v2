WHO COUNTS
Importers and distributors that buy packaged food or confectionery and resell it to retailers, pharmacies, convenience stores, foodservice or other trade customers in the market. Include import agents only if they also hold stock or manage distribution.

LEAVE OUT
Retailers that do not wholesale, brand owners, exporters, and logistics-only providers (3PLs, freight forwarders) that never buy the goods.

WHERE TO LOOK
Trade association member lists, trade show exhibitor lists, retailer supplier pages, industry press, company registries and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius), not only distributor directories. A directory listing alone is low confidence.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
A mid-size specialist health food distributor may outrank a large generalist. Prioritise: already imports European confectionery; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor; named access to the target retail channels; import licence and cold chain for chocolate.
