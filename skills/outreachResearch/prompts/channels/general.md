WHO COUNTS
Companies that match the channel "{{CATEGORY_AS_TYPED}}" as written, and that buy packaged food or confectionery to resell in the market. Read the channel's words literally. When unsure whether a company fits, leave it out or mark it low confidence.

LEAVE OUT
Brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and competitors that make sugar-free products.

WHERE TO LOOK
Trade association member lists, trade show exhibitor lists, industry press, company registries, retailer supplier pages and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). A directory listing alone is low confidence.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise: already handles European confectionery; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor; named access to retail, pharmacy, convenience or online channels in the market.
