matches: wholesal, cash and carry, cash & carry, buying group
---
WHO COUNTS
Wholesalers, cash and carry operators and wholesale buying groups that buy packaged food or confectionery, hold it in stock and sell it in bulk to retailers, convenience stores, pharmacies, foodservice or other trade buyers in the market. They may buy from importers rather than import directly. Wholesalers that run a symbol group or supply a network of independent convenience stores count fully.

LEAVE OUT
Retailers that sell only to consumers, brand owners, exporters and logistics-only providers. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock, even when they call themselves wholesalers or distributors. Pharmaceutical wholesalers whose main lines are medicines, medical equipment or generic pharmacy products. Companies that mainly sell their own products, their own brand or own label, or goods they make themselves. Suppliers of ingredients, raw materials or semi-finished products to professionals. Fine food, deli and single cuisine importers without branded confectionery or biscuits. Companies with no warehouse or depot of their own. Any company that sells nothing from Diablo's catalogue, or whose range you could not confirm.

WHERE TO LOOK
Start where trade buyers are listed: wholesale trade bodies, symbol group and buying group member lists, national importer and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass) and the country's grocery and convenience trade press. Also cash and carry directories, trade show exhibitor lists and company registries. Use a directory only to find a company, then confirm it on the company's own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a wholesaler that lists many branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits and snacks from other companies, holds them in its own warehouse or depot and supplies kiosks, independent grocers, convenience stores or vending operators. Prioritise, in this order: imports or lists European or UK branded confectionery and snacks; runs a symbol group or supplies many independent convenience, grocery or pharmacy stores; a mid-size, owner-run business where a decision maker can be reached; already carries sugar-free, no-added-sugar or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor; depots or strong regional coverage in the market.
