WHO COUNTS
Big distributors, importers and wholesalers that serve halal markets, Muslim shoppers and the shops that serve them, and that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks, hold them in their own warehouses and sell them on as they are to supermarkets, halal grocers, Turkish, Middle Eastern, North African and South Asian food shops and kiosks across a region or the whole country. Diablo holds a halal certificate, so these businesses can stock its range with confidence.

LEAVE OUT
Halal butchers, meat and poultry specialists, slaughterhouses and fresh meat wholesalers; halal certification bodies; restaurants, caterers and suppliers to kitchens; brand owners and makers of their own products; single halal shops and small traders (rule 12); and a world food wholesaler whose own website shows no sweets, biscuits or snacks section. Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
Halal food importer and wholesaler directories, member lists of the market's halal certification bodies (which list certified distributors), food importers' associations, Turkish, Arabic, Malay and Urdu language trade listings, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass) and the brand owners' distributor pages (SEARCH THROUGH THE BRANDS FIRST). Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a halal food importer or wholesaler with its own sweets and snacks section (branded biscuits, chocolate, wafers, gummies) that supplies many supermarkets and food shops, such as TATKO, the Turkish-Swiss wholesaler built on brands such as the biscuit maker Eti. Prioritise: national or regional reach; a clear sweets and snacks range from other brands; already carries halal certified confectionery.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rule 8: a halal specialist is not a cuisine specialist for this channel when its own website shows branded packaged sweets, biscuits or snacks for resale and it sells to shops; without such a section it is still left out. Rule 4: write how it holds stock after "Storage: ". Rule 7: halal meat, catering and restaurant suppliers are out. Rule 12: write what shows its size after "Scale: ". All the other rules apply in full.
