WHO COUNTS
Importers and distributors that buy packaged food or confectionery, hold it in stock and resell it to retailers, pharmacies, convenience stores, foodservice or other trade customers in the market. Mid-size, owner-run importers whose business is bringing foreign brands into the market count fully, including importers of British brands (for example those supplying British food shops for expatriates).

LEAVE OUT
Retailers that do not wholesale, brand owners, exporters and logistics-only providers (3PLs, freight forwarders) that never buy the goods. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers, import agents and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock, even when they call themselves distributors. Pharmaceutical wholesalers with no food or confectionery range. Any company whose food or confectionery range you could not confirm.

WHERE TO LOOK
Start where trade buyers are listed: national importer and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass) and the country's grocery, convenience and confectionery trade press. Also trade show exhibitor and visitor lists, retailer supplier pages, company registries and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). Use a directory only to find a company, then confirm it on the company's own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise, in this order: imports European or UK branded confectionery and snacks; a mid-size, owner-run importer where a decision maker can be reached; named access to retail, convenience or pharmacy chains in the market; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor; import licence and cold chain for chocolate. A mid-size specialist importer may outrank a large generalist.
