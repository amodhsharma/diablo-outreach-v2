matches: pharmacy and drugstore chain, drugstore chain, pharmacy chain, chemist chain
---
WHO COUNTS
Big pharmacy, chemist and drugstore chains that buy other companies' branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits, snack bars or other packaged food centrally and in bulk, into their own warehouse or distribution centre, and sell them in their stores across a region or the whole country (added 21 Nov 2026). Examples of the kind: Boots and Superdrug in the United Kingdom, Amavita and Sun Store in Switzerland, Chemist Warehouse and Priceline in Australia, Clicks and Dis-Chem in South Africa, dm and Rossmann in Germany, Watsons in Asia. Each must still pass every rule below on its own website.

LEAVE OUT
Single pharmacies and small groups; banner groups, franchise networks and buying groups where each pharmacy orders for itself and there is no central warehouse; online-only pharmacies; chains of fewer than about 100 stores without a national presence; chains that sell no food at all. A pharmacy with many branches but no central warehouse is out. Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
Rankings of the market's largest pharmacy and drugstore chains in the trade press, the chains' own websites, annual reports and store locators, their supplier or "become a supplier" pages, and their own online shops (the sweets, snacks and healthy food categories). Use a directory only to find a chain, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a national chain with hundreds of stores, a central distribution centre and a food, snacks or sugar-free shelf that already carries branded sweets, bars and biscuits from other companies, with a clear supplier route. Prioritise: store count and national reach; a central distribution centre; already stocks sugar-free or no-added-sugar confectionery without a direct competitor; already stocks European brands. Mention the store count and the distribution centre in the fit rationale.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rules 1 to 3: leave out chains that stock mainly their own label in food. Rule 4 for this channel: the central warehouse or distribution centre the chain buys into must be seen; write it after "Storage: " (a chain whose stores each order for themselves is out, "does not hold stock"). Rule 5: its own website or online shop must show that it already sells at least one category from DIABLO'S CATALOGUE from other brands. Rule 6: a pharmacy or drugstore chain counts when food is a real part of its shelves, even when medicines and personal care are its biggest lines. Rule 12 for this channel: about 100 stores or more, or a national presence, buying centrally; write the store count after "Scale: ". This channel keeps the full size rule: only big chains. All the other rules apply in full.
