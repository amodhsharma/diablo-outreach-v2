matches: supermarket, hypermarket, grocery chain, chain
---
WHO COUNTS
Supermarket and hypermarket chains, and grocery chains of that size, that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks centrally, from brands, importers or distributors, and sell them to shoppers in many stores across a region or the whole country. A chain's own online grocery shop counts as part of the chain.

LEAVE OUT
Single shops and small groups of a few stores, independent grocers, kiosks, convenience shops, sports shops, health food shops, pharmacies, speciality sweet shops and online-only shops: they are not part of this research. Also the marketplaces themselves, brand owners, discounters that stock only their own label, and non-food chains. Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
Rankings of the market's largest supermarket and grocery chains in the trade press, the chains' own websites and store locators, their supplier or "become a supplier" pages, retail association member lists and company registries. Check the chain's own online shop or category pages for the sweets, biscuits and snacks it already stocks.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a chain with many stores that already sells many branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits and snacks from other companies, ideally with a free from, sugar free or health shelf, and a clear supplier route (a supplier portal or "become a supplier" page). Prioritise: number of stores and reach; already stocks sugar-free or no-added-sugar confectionery without a direct competitor; already stocks European brands. Mention the store count in the fit rationale.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rules 1 to 3: leave out chains that stock mainly their own label. Rule 4: the chain buys stock and sells it from its own stores; no storage line is needed. Rule 5: its own website or online shop must show that it already sells at least one category from Diablo's catalogue from other brands. Rule 8: leave out gourmet and single-cuisine chains unless branded sweets, biscuits or snacks are a clear part of the shelf. Rule 12: at least about 10 stores, or a regional or national chain that buys centrally; write the store count after "Scale: ". Rules 9, 10 and 11 apply in full.
