matches: wholesal, cash and carry, cash & carry, buying group
---
WHO COUNTS
Wholesalers, cash and carry operators and wholesale buying groups that sell packaged food or confectionery in bulk to retailers, convenience stores, pharmacies, foodservice or other trade buyers in the market. They may buy from importers rather than import directly.

LEAVE OUT
Retailers that sell only to consumers, brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and brokers that never take stock.

WHERE TO LOOK
Wholesale trade bodies, cash and carry directories, buying group member lists, trade show exhibitor lists, industry press and company registries. A directory listing alone is low confidence.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise: already lists European or imported confectionery; already carries sugar-free, no-added-sugar or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor; a named retail, convenience or pharmacy customer base; depots or strong regional coverage in the market.
