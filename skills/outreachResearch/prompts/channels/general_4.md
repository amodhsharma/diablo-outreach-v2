WHO COUNTS
Companies that match the channel "{{CATEGORY_AS_TYPED}}" as written, and that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks, hold them in stock and resell them as they are in the market. Read the channel's words literally. When unsure whether a company fits, reject it.

LEAVE OUT
Brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and competitors that make sugar-free products. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock. Everything the HARD RULES leave out: own products or own label, kitchens and ingredients, delicatessen and cuisine specialists, pharmacy, drinks and coffee businesses, chilled and frozen specialists, no website, and anything that sells nothing from Diablo's catalogue.

WHERE TO LOOK
Trade association and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass), the country's grocery, kiosk and convenience trade press, trade show exhibitor lists, company registries, retailer supplier pages and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model is the one in WHO DIABLO WANTS. Prioritise, in this order: already sells branded confectionery and snacks from other companies, ideally European or UK brands; a mid-size business where a decision maker can be reached; named access to retail, kiosk, pharmacy, convenience or online channels in the market; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
All eleven apply as far as they fit the channel as typed. Rule 4: a company that sells to the trade must have its own warehouse or depot (write it after "Storage: " in the fit rationale); a company that sells to shoppers must buy stock and sell it from its own shops or online shop. When unsure whether a rule is met, reject the company.
