WHO COUNTS
Distributors, importers, wholesalers and shop chains that serve Muslim shoppers and the shops that serve them, and that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks, hold them in stock and sell them on as they are: to halal supermarkets and grocers, Turkish, Middle Eastern, North African and South Asian food shops, kiosks, or directly to shoppers in their own shops or online shop. Diablo holds a halal certificate, so these businesses can stock its range with confidence.

LEAVE OUT
Halal butchers, meat and poultry specialists, slaughterhouses and fresh meat wholesalers; halal certification bodies; restaurants, caterers and suppliers to kitchens; brand owners and makers of their own products; and a world food wholesaler whose own website shows no sweets, biscuits or snacks section. Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
Halal food importer and wholesaler directories, member lists of the market's halal certification bodies (which list certified distributors and shops), Muslim community business directories, Turkish, Arabic and Urdu language trade listings, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass) and local business listings for halal supermarkets. Search in the local language and in Turkish or Arabic where it helps. Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a halal food importer or wholesaler with its own sweets and snacks section (branded biscuits, chocolate, wafers, gummies) that supplies halal supermarkets and food shops, or a halal supermarket chain with a sweets aisle. In the Swiss review TATKO (a Turkish-Swiss wholesaler built on brands such as the biscuit maker Eti) and AM Food (a Balkan importer with its own snacks and sweets section) are the models. Prioritise: a clear sweets and snacks range from other brands; many shops supplied; already carries halal certified confectionery.

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rule 8: a halal specialist is not a cuisine specialist for this channel when its own website shows branded packaged sweets, biscuits or snacks for resale and it sells to shops or shoppers; without such a section it is still left out. Rule 4: write how it holds stock after "Storage: ". Rule 7: halal meat, catering and restaurant suppliers are out. All the other rules apply in full.
