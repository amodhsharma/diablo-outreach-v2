WHO COUNTS
Third-party sellers (companies, not individuals) based in or selling into the market that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits, snacks or health foods and resell them as they are through online marketplaces such as Amazon, Temu, Noon, Flipkart, Mercado Libre, Allegro, Bol, Shopee or Lazada, whichever operate there. They buy stock from brands or distributors and resell it, often on several marketplaces. One product is often sold by several such sellers.

LEAVE OUT
The marketplaces themselves, brand owners selling only their own brand, drop-shippers that hold no stock, individuals, and sellers whose main lines are not food. Everything the HARD RULES leave out, as HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY explains for sellers.

WHERE TO LOOK
Marketplace "sold by" and seller profile pages for branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits and snacks, seller ranking and analytics sites, the company registry entry behind the seller name, the seller's own website or company page, and local e-commerce trade press.

DOMAIN
Use the seller's own company website if it has one. A marketplace storefront page is not a domain: write "no domain" and give the storefront page as the source. This is the only channel where "no domain" is allowed.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model: a seller whose storefront already lists many branded sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks from other companies. Prioritise: already sells sugar-free, keto, vegan, diabetic-friendly or better-for-you snacks without a direct competitor; sells on several marketplaces; visible volume (ratings counts or best-seller ranks); already resells European brands; a legal company name that can be traced. Name the marketplaces each seller uses in "channels_supplied".

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rules 1 to 3: leave out sellers that sell only their own brand or goods they make. Rule 4: the seller must buy and hold its own stock (no drop-shippers, no sellers that only list other people's stock). Rule 5: its storefront must show that it already sells at least one category from Diablo's catalogue from other brands. Rule 6: leave out sellers whose main lines are not food (for example electronics, cosmetics, medicines, supplements or drinks). Rules 7, 8, 10 and 11 apply as written. Rule 9: open its own website, or its storefront when it has none, and check that it is still selling.
