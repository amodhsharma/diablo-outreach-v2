WHO COUNTS
Companies that match the channel "{{CATEGORY_AS_TYPED}}" as written, and that are big wholesalers, distributors, importers or chains: they buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks in volume, hold them in stock and resell them as they are to many shops or shoppers across a region or the whole country. Read the channel's words literally, but only within WHO DIABLO WANTS.

LEAVE OUT
Single shops and small businesses (rule 12), brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and competitors that make sugar-free products. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock. Everything else the HARD RULES leave out.

WHERE TO LOOK
The brand owners' distributor pages and the distributors' principals pages (SEARCH THROUGH THE BRANDS FIRST), trade association and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass), rankings in the trade press, trade show exhibitor lists, company registries and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The models are the ones in THE MODELS TO FOLLOW. Prioritise, in this order: reach (national or the leading regional player); already sells branded confectionery and snacks from other companies, ideally European or UK brands; named access to supermarket, convenience, pharmacy or kiosk chains; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor.
