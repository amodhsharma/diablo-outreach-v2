WHO COUNTS
Third-party sellers (companies, not individuals) based in or selling into the market that sell branded snacks, confectionery or health foods through online marketplaces such as Amazon, Temu, Noon, Flipkart, Mercado Libre, Allegro, Bol, Shopee or Lazada, whichever operate there. They buy stock from brands or distributors and resell it, often on several marketplaces. One product is often sold by several such sellers.

LEAVE OUT
The marketplaces themselves, brand owners selling only their own brand, drop-shippers that hold no stock, individuals, and sellers of non-food goods only. Companies that sell nothing from Diablo's catalogue, that mainly sell their own products or own label, or that supply ingredients and semi-finished products to professionals.

WHERE TO LOOK
Marketplace "sold by" and seller profile pages, seller ranking and analytics sites, the company registry entry behind the seller name, the seller's own website or company page, and local e-commerce trade press. A storefront page alone is low confidence.

DOMAIN
Use the seller's own company website if it has one. A marketplace storefront page is not a domain: write "no domain" and give the storefront page as the source.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise: already sells sugar-free, keto, diabetic-friendly or better-for-you snacks without a direct competitor; sells on several marketplaces; visible volume (ratings counts or best-seller ranks); already resells European brands; a legal company name that can be traced. Name the marketplaces each seller uses in "channels_supplied".

HOW THE HARD RULES APPLY
Rules 1 to 3: leave out sellers that sell only their own brand or goods they make. Rule 4: the seller must buy and hold its own stock (no drop-shippers, no sellers that only list other people's stock). Rule 5: it must already sell at least one category from Diablo's catalogue on its storefront. Rule 6: leave out sellers whose main lines are not food (for example electronics, cosmetics or medicines). Rules 7 and 8 apply as written. Rule 9: open its own website, or its storefront when it has none.
