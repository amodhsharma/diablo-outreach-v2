WHO COUNTS
Retailers that sell packaged snacks or confectionery to consumers in the market and buy directly from brands, importers or distributors: supermarket and grocery chains, health food and organic stores, pharmacy and drugstore chains, convenience chains, speciality sweet shops, department store food halls and online grocery retailers. Prefer chains and multi-store groups; include strong independents only when they buy direct.

LEAVE OUT
Single small shops with no buying function, the marketplaces themselves (for example Amazon or Temu), brand owners, restaurants and cafes, and retailers that only stock their own label.

WHERE TO LOOK
Retailer websites and store locators, supplier or "sell with us" pages, retail trade press, store opening news, chain rankings and company registries.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
Prioritise: already stocks sugar-free, no-added-sugar or better-for-you confectionery without a direct competitor; a clear supplier onboarding route (a supplier portal or "become a supplier" page); number of stores or online reach in the market; already stocks European brands. Mention the store count in the fit rationale when known.
