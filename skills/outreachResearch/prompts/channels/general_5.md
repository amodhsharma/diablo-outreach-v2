WHO COUNTS
Companies that match the channel "{{CATEGORY_AS_TYPED}}" as written, and that buy other companies' branded, packaged sweets, chocolate, biscuits or snacks, hold them in stock and resell them as they are in the market. Read the channel's words literally. Reject a company when what you see shows it breaks a rule; list it when it passes on what its own website shows, stating any smaller detail you could not see.

LEAVE OUT
Brand owners, exporters, logistics-only providers and competitors that make sugar-free products. Sales and marketing agencies, brokers and brand representation companies that do not buy and hold stock. Everything the HARD RULES leave out: own products or own label, kitchens and ingredients, delicatessen and cuisine specialists, medicine-led pharmacy businesses, drinks-only and coffee-led businesses, chilled and frozen specialists, no website, and anything that sells nothing from Diablo's catalogue.

WHERE TO LOOK
Trade association and supplier association member lists, importer directories (for example bestfoodimporters.com, Europages, Kompass), the country's grocery, kiosk and convenience trade press, trade show exhibitor lists, company registries, retailer supplier pages and import shipment databases (for example Volza, ImportGenius). Use a directory only to find a company, then check it on its own website.

WHAT GOOD FIT LOOKS LIKE
The model is the one in WHO DIABLO WANTS. Prioritise, in this order: already sells branded confectionery and snacks from other companies, ideally European or UK brands; a mid-size business where a decision maker can be reached; named access to retail, kiosk, pharmacy, convenience or online channels in the market; already carries sugar-free or better-for-you lines without a direct competitor.
