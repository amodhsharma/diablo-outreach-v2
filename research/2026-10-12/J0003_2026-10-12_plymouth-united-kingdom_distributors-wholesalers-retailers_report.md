## Market structure

Plymouth is served almost entirely through national route to market structures rather than a local import trade. Packaged confectionery reaches shelf in three ways: direct supply to the big multiples (Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Morrisons, Aldi, Lidl), which buy centrally; convenience wholesale, where symbol groups hold the largest share of UK convenience value (about 38.6% forecast for 2025, Lumina Intelligence) ahead of multiples, co-operatives, unaffiliated independents and forecourts; and specialist health food routes through national and South West natural food distributors into independents and chains such as Holland & Barrett. Local wholesale capacity is thin: Bestway closed its Plymouth depot in 2020 and moved customers to Exeter, so Plymouth independents rely on the local Booker branch, delivered wholesale from Exeter or Bristol depots and SPAR supply from the Saltash depot near Plymouth, which A.F. Blakemore has controlled since May 2026 (per the wholesaler research in this run). The real gatekeepers are central category buyers at the multiples and symbol wholesalers, and the buyers at a handful of specialist distributors; a Plymouth only deal will rarely bypass them. Online grocery is led by the same multiples.

## Closing analysis

- **A.F. Blakemore and Son (SPAR):** controls the Saltash SPAR depot serving 71 company stores and more than 130 independent SPAR stores in the South West, so one listing reaches the densest convenience network around Plymouth.
- **Redmoor:** a family owned health food wholesaler about 15 miles from Plymouth whose own fleet already delivers confectionery to independent health and farm shops in the city, making it the most natural first local partner.
- **Holland & Barrett:** the UK's main health food chain, with a Drake Circus store in Plymouth and national online reach, where shoppers already look for better for you snacks; a win here also builds credibility with the wholesalers above.

## Barriers to entry

- Listing payments: the Groceries Supply Code of Practice bars designated large grocers from requiring payment as a condition of listing, except for promotions or genuinely new products (not stocked in 25% or more of stores in the past year), where a risk based payment is allowed. Expect promotional and range review costs rather than a flat listing fee.
- Distributor margin and credit: typical UK distributor margins and payment terms could not be verified from a reliable source for this run; treat any figure as a negotiation point (unverified).
- Dominant players: the multiples and the big wholesalers and symbol groups (Booker, A.F. Blakemore SPAR, Bestway) control most shelf access in Plymouth; specialist health food distribution is concentrated in a few national players.
- HFSS rules (England): location restrictions on HFSS products at checkouts, aisle ends and entrances have applied since 1 October 2022 to businesses with 50 or more employees in stores over 2,000 sq ft; specialist retailers are exempt. Volume price restrictions (multibuys, BOGOF) were postponed. Whether each Diablo SKU is in scope depends on its nutrient profile score; check per product (not verified here).
- Sugar claims (GB retained Regulation 1924/2006): "sugars free" needs no more than 0.5 g sugars per 100 g; "with no added sugars" means no added mono or disaccharides or any other food used for its sweetening properties, plus "contains naturally occurring sugars" where relevant.
- Mandatory statements (GB retained Regulation 1169/2011, Annex III): "with sweetener(s)" next to the name; "excessive consumption may produce laxative effects" when added polyols exceed 10%. Diablo avoids aspartame, so the phenylalanine warning should not apply.
- Sweeteners: steviol glycosides (E 960) are on the GB domestic additives list, with further forms added in 2024; permitted uses and maximum levels by food category should be checked per SKU against the GB list.
- Import duty and border: Diablo sells through B Healthy Ltd (UK). Goods entering GB from the EU are zero tariff under the UK EU Trade and Cooperation Agreement only if they meet the rules of origin and carry an origin statement; otherwise UK Global Tariff rates apply. Where Diablo products are manufactured was not checked in this run. A UK EU SPS agreement is targeted for mid 2027 and is not yet in force, so current import controls still apply.
- Labelling: GB packs need a UK importer or food business address and English labelling; shelf life at entry norms could not be verified for this run.
