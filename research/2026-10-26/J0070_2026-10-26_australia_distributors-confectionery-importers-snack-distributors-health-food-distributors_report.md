## Market structure

UNVERIFIED: the shared web search budget for this run was used up by the Channel helpers before the Location research started, so this section is written from general knowledge without fresh sources and should be checked before use.

Grocery in Australia is highly concentrated: Woolworths and Coles hold the largest share, with Aldi third and Metcash supplying the independent IGA and Foodworks network. Packaged confectionery usually reaches the major chains either direct from large brand owners or through a specialist importer that holds the listing, stock and in store merchandising. Outside the majors, importers and wholesalers serve independent supermarkets, convenience and petrol stores, discount chains, health food shops and pharmacies, often state by state (Western Australia in particular is served by local wholesalers). Health food and sports nutrition distributors reach independent health shops, gyms and online retailers. Amazon Australia and specialist online health shops add an online route. The real gatekeepers are the category buyers at the majors and Metcash, and the importer who carries the brand for them.

## Barriers to entry

UNVERIFIED: written without fresh searches; confirm each point before relying on it.

- Major chain listings are hard to win and can involve promotional and marketing support costs; exact listing fee practice is unverified.

- Regulation: food must meet the Food Standards Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, including its rules on permitted sweeteners, nutrition content claims such as sugar free and no added sugar, and advisory statements for polyols; product specific details were not checked.
- Labelling: imported products need Australian compliant labels, including country of origin labelling where it applies; details not checked.
- Trade: an Australia UK free trade agreement is in force, which may reduce duty on UK goods; rates for this range were not checked.
- Imported food is subject to border inspection under the Imported Food Inspection Scheme (unverified for this range).
