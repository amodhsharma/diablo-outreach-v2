## Market structure

Unverified: the shared web search limit for this run was used up by the Channel searches before the Location research started, so this section rests on general background knowledge and was not checked against sources in this run. Treat every point as a lead to confirm.
South African grocery is concentrated in a few national chains (Shoprite and Checkers, Pick n Pay, Spar, Woolworths), which mostly buy through their own buying offices and distribution centres in Gauteng and the Western Cape, so national listings are decided centrally rather than in Cape Town. Health and pharmacy chains (Clicks, Dis-Chem) are a second gatekeeper for better-for-you snacks. Imported confectionery usually reaches these chains through an importer or distributor that handles customs, labelling and listings. Online, Takealot is the main marketplace and the chains run their own quick commerce apps (for example Checkers Sixty60). In Cape Town itself, independent health shops, sports and cycling shops, vending operators and corporate gifting businesses are reachable directly, as this run's Channel research shows.

## Barriers to entry

- Unverified: the points below were not checked against sources in this run because the search limit was reached; confirm each one before acting on it.
- Central buying at the major chains means listings, promotional support and possibly listing or rebate fees are negotiated nationally; terms are not published.
- Distributors typically expect a margin and credit terms on imported goods and may ask for exclusivity; figures are unverified.
- Labelling: foodstuffs fall under the Foodstuffs, Cosmetics and Disinfectants Act labelling regulations (R146); claims such as "sugar free" and "no added sugar" and sweetener and polyol warning statements must follow them. Newer draft labelling regulations have been proposed; their status is unverified.
- Sweeteners: stevia (steviol glycosides) and polyols must be permitted for each product type under South African sweetener rules; check each SKU.
- Import labelling must be in English with a local importer named on the pack (unverified detail); a sticker may be accepted.
- Trade: the SACU and Mozambique economic partnership agreement with the UK (and the equivalent with the EU) may lower duty on goods of UK or EU origin; duty rates per tariff line are unverified.
- The Health Promotion Levy applies to sugary drinks, not to confectionery, as far as known.
