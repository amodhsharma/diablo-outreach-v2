## Market structure

Australian grocery is highly concentrated. Coles and Woolworths hold the large majority of supermarket sales and buy centrally through their own distribution centres, so a ranging decision by their category buyers is the main gatekeeper for confectionery and snacks. Aldi is the third force with a mostly private label range. Independent supermarkets (IGA, Foodworks and others) are supplied largely through Metcash, which also serves convenience and route trade. Petrol and convenience stores ("servos") are an important impulse channel for confectionery, supplied by convenience wholesalers and the major confectionery companies' own field sales. Health food stores, pharmacies and gourmet grocers are reached through specialist importers and distributors, many based in Sydney and Melbourne. Online, Amazon Australia and retailer websites matter, but the share for imported confectionery is unverified. For a new imported brand the realistic first step is an importer or specialist distributor who holds stock, clears customs and labels, then sells into independents, health and convenience before approaching the two majors. Market shares in this section are general industry knowledge and were not verified with a figure in this run.

## Barriers to entry

- Two major chains dominate ranging; listing typically needs promotional funding and trade spend, but exact listing fees were not verified in this run.
- Many large Sydney distributors and wholesalers already carry competing confectionery and some work on exclusive brand agreements; terms vary and were not verified.
- Distributor margins and credit terms were not verified in this run; treat any figure as a question for the partner.
- Sweeteners and polyols must be permitted for the food category under the Food Standards Code (Standard 1.3.1 and Schedules); stevia glycosides are permitted, but each SKU should be checked against its category.
- Foods with added polyols above set levels carry a mandatory advisory statement that excess consumption may have a laxative effect.
- Sugar-free and no-added-sugar claims follow Standard 1.2.7 conditions; the exact thresholds should be checked per SKU before labelling.
- Imported packs need Australian labelling: an Australian importer name and address, a Nutrition Information Panel in Australian format, allergen declarations in the PEAL format and country of origin labelling.
- Shelf life on arrival matters because of sea freight time; partner requirements were not verified.
- The Australia UK Free Trade Agreement, in force since May 2023, removed tariffs on most UK goods, which helps B Healthy Ltd; rules of origin must be met.
