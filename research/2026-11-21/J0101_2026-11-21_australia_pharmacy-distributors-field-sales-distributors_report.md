## Market structure

In Australian pharmacy, front of shop lines such as confectionery reach the shelf through a few full line pharmaceutical wholesalers that serve almost every community pharmacy, supported by the government's Community Service Obligation funding pool for medicines distribution. Sigma Healthcare, now merged with Chemist Warehouse, is the largest; the other CSO wholesalers are reported to include Symbion and CH2, but their current shares were not verified in this run. Outside pharmacy, convenience, petrol and independent grocery are served by Metcash (which also bought the confectionery field sales network The Distributors), Campbells and regional field sales distributors whose reps call on milk bars, service stations and newsagents. Most of these large players are already in Diablo's contact list, so few new names came up. Supermarkets (Woolworths, Coles, Aldi) remain the main route for packaged confectionery overall but were not part of this job.

## Barriers to entry

- Concentration: a few pharmacy wholesalers and Metcash in convenience control access to most outlets; distributor margins, listing fees and credit norms were not verified in this run.
- Field sales: The Distributors network is now owned by Metcash, which narrows the independent field sales options.
- Claims: sugar free and no added sugar claims must meet the conditions in Standard 1.2.7 of the Food Standards Code (FSANZ); check the exact wording before relabelling.
- Labelling: Australian labels (FSANZ Code, country of origin rules) differ from UK labels, so a local sticker or pack is likely needed; polyol advisory statements apply (thresholds not verified here).
- Sweeteners: stevia and inulin are permitted under the Code; each polyol and its use level by food category should be checked.
- Duty: the Australia and United Kingdom Free Trade Agreement removes tariffs on UK originating goods, subject to rules of origin; imported food also falls under the Imported Food Inspection Scheme.
Sources: [Standard 1.2.7 Nutrition, health and related claims](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2015L00394/2025-08-13/2025-08-13/text/original/pdf), [UK government: Australia food and drink sector explainer](https://www.business.gov.uk/markets/australia/sector-explainers/food-and-drink/), [Austrade: A-UK FTA](https://www.austrade.gov.au/australian/export/free-trade-agreements/aukfta)
