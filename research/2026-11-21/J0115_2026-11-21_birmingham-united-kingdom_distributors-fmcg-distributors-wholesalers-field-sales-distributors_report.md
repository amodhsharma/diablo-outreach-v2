## Market structure

Packaged confectionery and snacks reach Birmingham shoppers through the same two routes as the rest of Great Britain. The big grocers (Tesco, Sainsbury's, Asda, Aldi, Lidl, Morrisons and Ocado online) buy centrally into their own national depots, so a Birmingham listing with them is a national listing decided by central category buyers. The independent and convenience sector (symbol group stores, independents, forecourts and a large South Asian grocery trade) is supplied by delivered and cash and carry wholesalers; Hancocks and Parfetts both run Birmingham depots, and Booker and Bestway serve the West Midlands, alongside specialist confectionery wholesalers such as Monmore. The gatekeepers are the grocers' central buyers and the wholesalers' buying teams and buying groups. Diablo is itself UK based (B Healthy Ltd), so the task is winning listings, not importing. ## Barriers to entry

- Concentrated wholesale: a handful of wholesalers and buying groups control access to most independent and symbol stores; listings usually need promotional funding (amounts unverified).
- Grocer listings: central buying, set range review windows and supplier funded promotions; fees and terms are confidential and not verified here.
- HFSS rules (England): location and, since October 2025, volume price promotion restrictions apply to in scope products in larger stores; each SKU needs a nutrient profile check.
- Sweeteners: GB assimilated Regulation 1333/2008 sets permitted sweeteners and polyols by food category.
- Laxative warning: more than 10% added polyols requires "excessive consumption may produce laxative effects" (Regulation 1169/2011 Annex III as assimilated in GB).
- Claims: "sugar-free" and "no added sugars" must meet the GB nutrition claims conditions.
- Duty: none for a UK company selling in Birmingham; goods made in the EU need a GB importer address on pack (not checked per product).
Sources: [legislation.gov.uk: Regulation 1169/2011 Annex III](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/eur/2011/1169/annex/III), [legislation.gov.uk: Regulation 1333/2008 labelling](https://legislation.gov.uk/eur/2008/1333/chapter/IV?view=plain), [GOV.UK: HFSS promotions guidance](https://gov.uk/government/publications/restricting-promotions-of-products-high-in-fat-sugar-or-salt-by-location-and-by-volume-price/restricting-promotions-of-products-high-in-fat-sugar-or-salt-by-location-and-by-volume-price-implementation-guidance)
