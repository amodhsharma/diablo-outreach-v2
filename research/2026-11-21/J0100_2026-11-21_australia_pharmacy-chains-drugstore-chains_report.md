## Market structure

In Australian pharmacy, confectionery and snacks reach the shelf mainly through a few large chains and banner groups. Chemist Warehouse is the dominant discount pharmacy retailer and, since its merger with the wholesaler Sigma Healthcare (cleared by the ACCC subject to an undertaking), it sits inside a group that also supplies other pharmacy banners. Priceline (owned by Wesfarmers) and the TerryWhite Chemmart network are the other large banners. Many banner pharmacies are independently owned and buy through the banner's or a wholesaler's ordering system rather than from a single chain warehouse, so the banner head office and the full line wholesalers are the real gatekeepers for front of shop ranging. Most of the large chains are already in Diablo's contact list, which is why this run found no new names. Supermarkets (Woolworths, Coles, Aldi) remain the main route for packaged confectionery generally; they were not part of this job.

## Barriers to entry

- Concentration: the Chemist Warehouse and Sigma group controls a large share of both pharmacy retail and wholesale; ranging there is the single biggest gate. Listing fees and margin norms were not verified in this run.
- Banner groups: independents under a banner often buy only what the banner ranges, so a listing with the banner office matters more than single stores.
- Claims: nutrition content claims such as sugar free and no added sugar must meet the conditions in Standard 1.2.7 of the Food Standards Code (FSANZ); the exact wording should be checked against the current standard before relabelling.
- Labelling: Australian labelling (FSANZ Code, country of origin rules) differs from UK labels, so a local sticker or pack is likely needed; polyol advisory statements apply under the Code (exact thresholds not verified here).
- Sweeteners: stevia (steviol glycosides) and inulin are permitted under the Code, but each polyol and its use level by food category should be checked.
- Duty: the Australia and United Kingdom Free Trade Agreement removes tariffs on UK originating goods, subject to rules of origin; imported food is also subject to the Imported Food Inspection Scheme.
