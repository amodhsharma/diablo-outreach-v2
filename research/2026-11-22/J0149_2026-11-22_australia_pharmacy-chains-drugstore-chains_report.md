## Market structure

Packaged confectionery and snacks in Australia reach shelf mainly through the two big supermarket chains, Woolworths and Coles, with Aldi and the IGA independents (supplied by Metcash) behind them. In pharmacy, the gatekeepers are the full line pharmacy wholesalers: Sigma Healthcare (merged with Chemist Warehouse in 2025), Symbion (part of EBOS Group) and Australian Pharmaceutical Industries (owned by Wesfarmers, which runs Priceline). Most community pharmacies trade under banner or buying groups that set a central range and promotions, then order stock through these wholesalers, so a listing usually needs both the banner group's range approval and a wholesaler code. Chemist Warehouse buys centrally and is the largest single pharmacy retailer. Front of shop snacks in pharmacy lean towards health, diabetic friendly and sports nutrition lines. Online, the chains' own web shops and Amazon Australia matter for niche better for you brands. Exact shares by channel were not verified in this run.

## Barriers to entry

- Concentration: a few wholesalers and banner groups control the pharmacy range; listing in one banner rarely opens another.
- Listing and promotional fees: banner groups and supermarkets commonly ask for range fees, catalogue support and rebates; exact levels unverified.
- Margins and credit: wholesaler margin plus pharmacy retail margin must both be funded; 30 to 60 day terms are typical but unverified for this range.
- Sweeteners: steviol glycosides, inulin and the common polyols are permitted under the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code; product by product checks against Schedule 15 are still needed.
- Laxative warning: foods with added polyols above the Code's threshold need an advisory statement that excess consumption may have a laxative effect.
- Claims: "sugar free" and "no added sugar" are nutrition content claims with set conditions in the Code (Standard 1.2.7); UK pack wording may not match and needs checking.
- Labelling: an Australian importer or supplier name and address, English labelling, country of origin rules and a nutrition information panel in the Australian format are required, so a sticker or local pack is likely.
- Imported food checks: shipments fall under the Imported Food Inspection Scheme; shelf life at entry expectations from buyers were not verified.
- Duty: the Australia United Kingdom Free Trade Agreement (in force since May 2023) removes tariffs on UK origin goods that meet its rules of origin; 10% GST still applies to confectionery.
