## Market structure

London is a city job inside the wider United Kingdom research, so this section is kept short. In London, packaged confectionery reaches independent and convenience shops mainly through wholesalers and cash and carries (Booker, Bestway and many independent London wholesalers listed in the Unitas directory), symbol and franchise groups, and delivered impulse distributors that run van sales and field sales into corner shops, forecourts and newsagents. Supermarkets and the major multiples buy centrally from their own head offices and distribution centres, so a London city search mostly adds independent wholesalers and delivered distributors. This run's web search budget ran out before the city could be researched in depth, so market shares and the relative weight of each route were not verified.

## Barriers to entry

- HFSS rules in England restrict location and volume price promotion of less healthy foods in larger stores; whether each Diablo product scores as less healthy under the nutrient profiling model needs checking product by product.
- Polyol sweetened foods must carry the statement "excessive consumption may produce laxative effects" under retained Regulation 1169/2011 annex III; sweeteners are governed by retained Regulation 1333/2008.
- Diablo is a UK company, so no import duty or customs barrier applies in London.
- Listing fees, distributor margins and credit terms were not verified in this run.
