## Market structure

Packaged confectionery and snacks in South Africa reach shelf mainly through the big grocery groups (Shoprite Checkers, Pick n Pay, SPAR, Woolworths) and a large informal trade of spaza shops supplied by wholesalers and cash and carry. Pharmacy retail is led by two national chains, Clicks and Dis-Chem, which buy centrally and run their own distribution; Clicks also owns UPD, a major pharmaceutical wholesaler, so one group controls both a chain and a wholesale route. The rest of retail pharmacy is made up of independents trading under franchise or banner groups such as Alpha Pharm, Link and TLC, plus regional groups such as Arrie Nel and supermarket pharmacies such as Medirite and Pharmacy at SPAR. Front of shop food in pharmacy leans towards health, diabetic friendly and sports nutrition lines. Recent press reports a push to consolidate independent pharmacies; current share figures by chain were not verified in this run.

## Barriers to entry

- Concentration: Clicks and Dis-Chem hold most chain pharmacy shelf space and buy centrally; banner groups set ranges but rarely hold stock.
- Listing and promotional fees: national chains commonly ask for listing fees, promotional support and rebates; exact levels unverified.
- Margins and credit: chains and wholesalers usually expect long payment terms; figures for this range were not verified.
- Sweeteners: permitted sweeteners and their use levels are set by the Sweeteners in Foodstuffs regulations (R733 of 2012); stevia, inulin and polyol use needs checking product by product.
- Laxative warning: the labelling rules require a warning on foods with added polyols above set levels; the exact wording and threshold should be confirmed.
- Claims: "sugar free" and "no added sugar" claims are controlled under the labelling regulations (R146, with later amendments and a draft revision); UK pack wording may not comply.
- Labelling: a South African importer name and address and local format labelling are required, so a sticker or local pack is likely.
- Shelf life at entry: buyer expectations were not verified.
- Duty: the SACUM UK Economic Partnership Agreement gives UK origin goods preferential access to the SACU market; the exact rate for confectionery should be checked in the SACUM preferential tariff reference; VAT of 15% applies.
