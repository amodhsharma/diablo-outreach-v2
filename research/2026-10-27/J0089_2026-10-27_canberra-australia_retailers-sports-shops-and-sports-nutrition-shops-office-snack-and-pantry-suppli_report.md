## Closing analysis

- The Fruit Box Group first: a national office pantry supplier that lists Canberra among its delivery areas and already sells sweet biscuits, confectionery and snack boxes from many suppliers to more than 8,000 customers.
- Mountain Creek Wholefoods second: a Canberra health food store with more than 8,000 products from about 200 suppliers and its own chocolate, sweets and snacks category, so sugar-free chocolate and biscuits fit its shelf.
- Gourmet by Design third: a Canberra hamper house since 2004 that buys in branded biscuits and chocolate for corporate and private hampers, a natural home for Diablo's gift boxes ahead of Christmas.
