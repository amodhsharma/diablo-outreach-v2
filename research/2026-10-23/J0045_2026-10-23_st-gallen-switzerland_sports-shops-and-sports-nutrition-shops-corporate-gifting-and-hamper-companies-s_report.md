## Market structure

Swiss grocery is held by two cooperatives. Figures reported in November 2025 put Coop ahead of Migros in food and near-food sales, so their central buying desks are the main gatekeepers for any branded snack, with Denner, Lidl and Aldi behind them. St. Gallen is German speaking and is the seat of Genossenschaft Migros Ostschweiz, the regional cooperative for Eastern Switzerland; how much listing power sits with the regional cooperative rather than the national buying desk was not verified. For the channels in this job, specialist product reaches shelf through Swiss importers and wholesalers that list foreign brands, national sports nutrition chains and Swiss online shops (most already known to Diablo) and a small number of independent shops. Gifting and hamper firms in Eastern Switzerland lean on Swiss regional and artisanal products, and seasonal and gift shops are led by confectioners selling their own production and by chains already on the exclusions list (observation from this run's research, not a published figure).

## Barriers to entry

- Two cooperatives control most grocery shelf space and push own label hard; listing terms and fees are not public (unverified).
- Distributor margin, credit and exclusivity norms could not be verified in this run; expect importers to ask for national exclusivity (unverified).
- Gift and hamper buyers in Eastern Switzerland favour Swiss regional and artisanal products, so a UK brand must win on the sugar-free angle rather than origin (observation, not a published figure).
- UK goods: the UK Switzerland trade agreement says UK processed agricultural goods face tariffs no higher than EU goods; rates must be checked per tariff line, and preferential origin needs proof of origin.
- Swiss food law broadly follows EU rules on sweeteners, polyols and nutrition claims, including a laxative warning where added polyols exceed 10%; the exact Swiss ordinance wording was not verified in this run.
- Labels must be in at least one Swiss official language (German is the practical choice for St. Gallen); other Swiss specific labelling details were not verified.
- Shelf life at entry and fees in the gifting and seasonal channels: not verified.
