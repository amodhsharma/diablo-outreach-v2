## Market structure

Swiss grocery is held by two cooperatives, Coop and Migros, with Denner, Lidl and Aldi behind them, so their central buying desks remain the main gatekeepers for any branded snack. Lucerne is German speaking and is served by Genossenschaft Migros Luzern, the regional cooperative for Central Switzerland, which has been expanding its store network and stresses regional products; how much listing power sits with the regional cooperative rather than the national buying desk was not verified. For the channels in this job, specialist product reaches shelf through Swiss importers and wholesalers that list foreign brands, national sports nutrition chains and Swiss online shops (most already known to Diablo) and a small number of independent shops. Lucerne gifting and hamper firms that use branded sweets are already known to Diablo; the rest build boxes from regional and artisanal products, and seasonal and gift shops are led by confectioners selling their own production and by chains already on the exclusions list. This run found very few independent Lucerne companies new to Diablo in these channels.

## Closing analysis

- **Triple X Fitness Shop GmbH (online-nutrition.ch):** the only tier 1 company; a Lucerne city centre sports nutrition store with an online shop that already stocks other brands' bars, cookies, chocolate, spreads and sugar-free syrups, so Diablo's range fits its existing shelf categories.
- **HarderSupps:** tier 2 sports nutrition shop already selling cookies, oat bars, chocolate drops, spreads and flavour syrups from other brands, a close match for Diablo's biscuits, spreads and syrups.
- **Changemaker:** tier 2 Lucerne gift shop selling chocolate, cookies, spreads and bars in gift boxes from other brands plus a Christmas range, a natural home for Diablo's seasonal and gift lines.

## Barriers to entry

- Two cooperatives control most grocery shelf space and push own label hard; listing terms and fees are not public (unverified).
- Distributor margin, credit and exclusivity norms could not be verified in this run; expect importers to ask for national exclusivity (unverified).
- Gift and hamper buyers in Lucerne favour Swiss regional and artisanal products, so a UK brand must win on the sugar-free angle rather than origin (observation from this run, not a published figure).
- UK goods: the UK Switzerland trade agreement says UK processed agricultural goods face tariffs no higher than EU goods; rates must be checked per tariff line, and preferential origin needs proof of origin.
- Swiss food law broadly follows EU rules on sweeteners, polyols and nutrition claims, including a laxative warning where added polyols exceed 10%; the exact Swiss ordinance wording was not verified in this run.
- Labels must be in at least one Swiss official language (German is the practical choice for Lucerne); other Swiss specific labelling details were not verified.
- Shelf life at entry and fees in the gifting and seasonal channels: not verified.
