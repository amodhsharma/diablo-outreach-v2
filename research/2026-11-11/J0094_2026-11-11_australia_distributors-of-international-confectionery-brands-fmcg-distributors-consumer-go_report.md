## Market structure

UNVERIFIED THIS RUN: the shared web search limit was used up by the Channel helpers before this section could be researched, so it is written from general knowledge and every point needs checking.
Packaged confectionery in Australia reaches shelf mainly through the two big supermarket chains, Woolworths and Coles, followed by Aldi, the independent grocery network supplied by Metcash (IGA and others) and the convenience and petrol channel. The supermarket buyers are the real gatekeepers: a national listing usually needs a local distributor or broker who handles ranging reviews, promotions and logistics into each chain's distribution centres. Imported brands usually work through a specialist importer and distributor, or a sales and merchandising broker, rather than shipping straight to the chains. Convenience, petrol and independent stores are reached through route trade wholesalers and a national convenience wholesaler. Health food stores and pharmacies are a separate, smaller channel with their own wholesalers. Online, the supermarkets' own sites and Amazon Australia matter more than pure play marketplaces (unverified).

## Barriers to entry

- UNVERIFIED THIS RUN: written without searches; check every point before relying on it.
- Supermarket concentration: Woolworths and Coles hold most grocery sales, so ranging reviews, promotional funding and trading terms are the main commercial barrier (figures not verified).
- Distributor norms: exclusive national distribution for a brand is common; distributor margin and payment terms were not verified in this run.
- Sweeteners: steviol glycosides, inulin and polyols such as maltitol and erythritol are permitted food additives under the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code, but permissions by product type were not checked in this run.
- Claims: "sugar free" and "no added sugar" are nutrition content claims under Standard 1.2.7 with set conditions; the exact conditions were not verified in this run.
- Laxative warning: foods with added polyols above a set level need an advisory statement about excess consumption having a laxative effect (threshold not verified in this run).
- Labelling: imported products need an Australian importer or supplier name and address on pack and country of origin labelling rules apply (details not verified).
- Trade: the Australia UK free trade agreement removed tariffs on most UK goods; entry into force date and any confectionery specifics were not verified in this run.
