## Market structure

Note: the web search limit for this run was used up by the Channel helpers before the Location research started, so this section is written without fresh searches and every point below is unverified.

Packaged confectionery in South Africa is understood to reach shelf mainly through a small number of national grocery groups (such as Shoprite Checkers, Pick n Pay, Spar and Woolworths) that buy centrally into their own distribution centres, alongside wholesalers and cash and carry groups that serve independent stores and the informal spaza trade. Gauteng, with Johannesburg at its centre, is widely seen as the commercial hub where most national distributors and buying offices sit. Imported brands usually enter through an importer or appointed distributor who handles clearance, labelling and the listing process, with field sales teams covering independent and forecourt stores. Pharmacy and health chains such as Clicks and Dis-Chem are a likely route for sugar-free ranges (unverified). Online grocery is growing through the retailers' own apps rather than third party marketplaces (unverified).
